Pros and cons of agile: Allows for a dynamic change of requirements, and value is added to the customer after every sprint. Cons: Since customer is allowed to change requirements, tasks may frequently be added back to the backlog. Also, you have a limit on how many tasks you can have at a time, which can possibly be an issue

A product manager deals more with the stakeholders, and  manages the release of the products. The scrum master deals more with the development team and sprint planning.

Sprint planning meeting: 

Figure out what needs to be accomplished for the sprint.

Assign the different story points to different members of the team

Daily Scrum meeting:

Discuss development plans for the day to contribute to the sprint

Usually relatively short

Retrospective meeting:

End of sprint meeting to discuss completed work

Move incomplete work back to the backlog
